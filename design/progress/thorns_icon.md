## What is actually there

A saturated green sphere with six red-tipped brown spikes radiating outward,
two of them visibly clearing the ball's surface at bottom and top-right per
`ART-REVIEW.md`'s own build note on the rebuild that fixed buried spikes.

- **Silhouette @ 42px (9):** the spiked-ball shape stays completely legible
  at 42px — every spike is individually visible and the red tips read as
  distinct accent points, not a smudge. The cleanest silhouette of the
  batch.
- **Family distinction (10):** nothing else in this batch or its named
  neighbours (`shield`/`guard`/`wall`) is round, green, or spiked — the
  single most unambiguous line scored across all four icons.
- **Mechanic match (8):** "a landed attack reflects damage back" — a ball
  that will hurt whatever touches it is an immediate, near-universal visual
  metaphor, and the red spike tips read as "the part that hurts you"
  clearly even at 42px. The strongest mechanic read in the batch.
- **Colour & contrast (9):** saturated green against the brown card standin
  is the highest-contrast pairing scored under this item since the beast
  and portrait batches; the red tips add a second accent that separates
  from both the green body and the brown card.
- **Style consistency (7):** the green body is a genuine palette outlier
  against the blue/grey/steel family the other three defensive icons (and
  `shield`/`guard`/`wall`) share — presumably deliberate, since Thorns is a
  damage-reflection keyword rather than a Block-family one, but it does mean
  this icon reads as visually apart from its own review block rather than
  a fourth member of one family.

## Diagnosis — two lowest

1. **Style consistency (7).** Not proposing a colour change — this item
   scores and proposes, and repainting an icon to match a family it may not
   conceptually belong to is exactly the kind of art-direction call that's
   Nick's, not a fix to hand him pre-decided. Flagging the outlier is the
   deliverable here, not a swatch.
2. **Mechanic match (8, tied-lowest-of-the-strong-lines).** Concrete fix:
   none needed — the read is already strong; noted only because every other
   line scored higher.

Not applying either — this item scores and proposes; a fix is Nick's call.
